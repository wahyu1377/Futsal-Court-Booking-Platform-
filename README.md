# Arena Sport - Futsal Court Booking Platform

Arena Sport is a modern digital platform designed to simplify searching, booking, and paying for futsal courts in real-time. This platform aims to integrate real-time court schedule availability and digital payment systems, featuring a flagship **Split Bill** functionality to automatically and transparently divide rental fees among all team members.
